# Guitar chord-position data

The guitar chord positions in `guitar-positions.json` are a trimmed subset of **[@tombatossals/chords-db](https://github.com/tombatossals/chords-db)**, specifically its `lib/guitar.json` dataset. The dataset supplies standard-tuning fretting positions, finger assignments, base-fret offsets, and barre information. WorshipFlow applies chord-tone validation and renders the positions in its own UI.
